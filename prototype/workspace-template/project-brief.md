# Q4 client portal launch

Demo data for the Safe-Mode Gateway prototype.

## Goal
Launch the self-serve client portal by 15 December, so clients can find their invoices and contracts without emailing us.

## Scope
- Invoices and contracts in one place
- Single sign-on for client teams
- Download history for the finance team

## Timeline
- 1 December: beta with three clients
- 15 December: launch to every client
